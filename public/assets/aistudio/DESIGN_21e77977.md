## Brand & Style

The design system embodies radical discipline, precision, and austere technical clarity. Created for high-velocity quantitative surveillance, intelligence parsing, and real-time sentiment analysis, the interface strips away all chromatic noise to prioritize uncompromised data fidelity and cognitive bandwidth.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Disciplined Monochromacy:** Absolute rejection of semantic neons and arbitrary dashboard colorations. State is communicated exclusively via luminous luminance steps, surface elevation contrasts, and crisp typographical hierarchy.
- **Architectural Haarlines:** Structural boundaries rely on hairline borders (1px) and matte slate planes rather than heavy shadows or decorative separators.
- **Precision Density:** High informational density tempered by deliberate internal spatial padding, ensuring expansive analytical surfaces remain legible, calm, and breathable under high-frequency metric throughput.
- **Instrument Tactility:** Every micro-interaction feels weighted, immediate, and calibrated—similar to physical aerospace telemetry panels or luxury mechanical chronograph readouts.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising tri-tonal rule: Deep Matte Blacks, Structural Neutral Grays, and Radiant Stark Whites. No ambient saturations or chromatic tints are permitted.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Luminous Pure White / `#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for focal data values, primary navigational labels, key metric readouts, and active structural indicators. Light step `#F4F5F6` serves as an interactive hover state.
- **Secondary (Neutral Grays):**
  - Text & Accents: `#A1A8B2` (secondary labels, metric deltas), `#8E96A0` (structural micro-copy, timestamps).
  - Borders & Hairlines: `#3F444D` (active/focused hairlines), `#26292E` (standard dividing lines), `#22252A` (default hairline boundary).
  - Subtle Layers: `#181A1D` (elevated cards, pill badges, interactive input wells).
- **Base (Deep Matte Black):**
  - Canvas Root: `#090A0B` (void background).
  - Recessed Containers / Structural Panels: `#0E1012` (workbench modules, side rails, nested panels).

### Chromatic Governance
- Quantitative increases or decreases must not use red/green signifiers. Represent polarity using directional arrowheads (`↑`, `↓`), mathematical signs (`+`, `-`), or optical fill weight differences (e.g., solid stark white badge vs. hairline muted gray outline).
- Focus states and active toggles use 100% white optical opacity rather than color washes.

## Typography

Typography balances clinical legibility with mechanical precision:
- **Structural Text (Geist):** Handles titles, analytical summaries, entity definitions, and metadata narrative. Tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.04em`) produces a razor-sharp modernist density.
- **Telemetry & Numerical Readouts (JetBrains Mono):** Drives all timestamps, volumetric scores, financial shifts, rate trackers, coordinates, and delta states. Tabular figures (`tnum`) and slashed zeros are strictly enforced to prevent tabular layout shifts during streaming data re-renders.
- **Hierarchy Standard:** Avoid decorative italicization. Distinguish primary numbers (`stat-mono-xl`) from auxiliary trends (`label-mono-sm`) via spatial separation and luminance transitions from `#FFFFFF` down to `#8E96A0`.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances dense information arrays with generous internal breathing room to eliminate cognitive strain.

### Grid & Structure
- **Modular Fluid Grid:** 12-column variable grid on desktop with structured column anchoring; 4-column system on mobile devices.
- **Breathing Strategy:** Component tiles and metrics modules maintain generous internal padding (`space-lg` / `24px`) around data points, preventing telemetry values from visual collision even when screens are densely populated with tables.
- **Reflow & Boundaries:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** Permanent left-hand collapsed rail (64px) expanding to analytical navigation (240px); fluid modular analytical cards spanning 3, 4, 6, or 12 columns.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, responsive card wrapping into two vertical stacks, contextual drilldown side-drawers transition to full 50vw modals.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream, drilldowns become bottom-anchored sheets (90vh height).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and razor-thin boundaries rather than ambient light drops or blurred diffusion.

### The Low-Luminance Stacking Model
- **Base Level 0 (System Root):** `#090A0B` matte flat background.
- **Level 1 (Module Canvas):** `#0E1012` container surfaces with a mandatory border of `1px solid #22252A`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Panels & Flyouts):** `#181A1D` elevated panels, context menus, and toolbars, bounded by `1px solid #26292E`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Telemetry Drawers):** `#0E1012` surface with an outer rim of `1px solid #3F444D` backed by an absolute blackout scrim (`#000000` at 70% opacity, without blur) to isolate deep analysis.

### Micro-Shadows
Ambient shadows are strictly avoided across all static states. Modals and context menus employ a sharp, tight proximity drop: `0px 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`. Hover states never cast drop shadows; they assert depth through razor border highlight transitions (from `#22252A` to `#3F444D`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained Soft shape radius (Scale: `1`), producing clipped, engineered corners that harmonize with data grids.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Micro UI (Badges, Pills, Buttons):** `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px). Small, structured, and precise.
- **Containers & Metric Cards:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px). Creates a distinct container perimeter without leaning into friendly consumer bubbles.
- **Context Overlays & Drawers:** `rounded-lg` (8px) on dialog corners; drawers flush to edges feature 0px corner radii on adjoining viewports.
- **Hairlines:** Uniform stroke thickness of 1px across all component vectors, icons, and separators.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary:** Stark White (`#FFFFFF`) surface with deep black text (`#090A0B`), medium weight (`Geist`, 500). Hover triggers pure `#F4F5F6` with instant 100ms ease.
- **Secondary / Neutral:** `#181A1D` background, `1px solid #26292E`, text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover brightens hairline border to `#3F444D`.
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, text in `#8E96A0`, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on hover.

### 2. Analytical Metric Cards (Collapsible & Zoom-Revealed)
- **Resting:** Background `#0E1012`, `1px solid #22252A`, internal padding `1.5rem`. Contains categorical label (`label-mono-sm` in `#8E96A0`), primary numerical readout (`stat-mono-xl` in `#FFFFFF`), and directional shift indicator.
- **Collapse Mode:** Header chevron trigger collapses the card height to `48px`, truncating details and displaying only the condensed title and trailing total metric.
- **Hover Micro-Zoom (Detail Inspection):** Hovering over sparklines or data modules activates a localized `transform: scale(1.015)` combined with a hairline transition to `1px solid #3F444D`. Auxiliary metadata (sample size, moving averages) fades in smoothly (`opacity: 1`, 150ms) within the card footer.

### 3. Chips & Status Badges
- **Format:** Tight padding (`2px 6px`), JetBrains Mono (`code-badge`, 10px), uppercase.
- **Default Neutral:** Background `#181A1D`, hairline `1px solid #26292E`, text `#A1A8B2`.
- **Active / Flagged State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#090A0B`, no border. Provides instant, high-contrast focal points across log streams.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** `#0E1012` base with inset feel, border `1px solid #22252A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Placeholder:** `#3F444D` tracking monospace or clean sans.
- **Focus:** Crisp hairline switch to `1px solid #FFFFFF`. No glow, no chromatic ring.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selection
- **Form:** 14px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), border `1px solid #3F444D`, background transparent.
- **Selected State:** Checked surface fills with `#FFFFFF`; mark or radio inner pip rendered in `#090A0B`.

### 6. Lists & Log Feeds
- **Structure:** Tabular rows, height 40px, separated by a bottom hairline (`1px solid #181A1D`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover invokes instant full-row surface shift to `#141619`. Monospace values align right; entity titles align left.

### 7. Drilldown Drawers & Detail Modals
- **Drawer Drilldown:** Slides from right viewport edge (width: 480px desktop, 100vw mobile). Frame background `#0E1012`, left structural hairline `1px solid #26292E`. Features granular event logs, historical audit trails, and raw JSON inspectors.
- **Inspector Modal:** Centered dialog, `640px` max-width, internal padding `2rem`, framed in `1px solid #3F444D`.
